# Car games

| Game | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Dirt Sleeper](dirt-sleeper/) | Build one stock-looking luxury SUV into a dirt-drag sleeper or a mud rig, then race it on a ranch. |
| [Dirt Sleeper Mini](dirt-sleeper-mini/) | Seven cars and their builds (an SUV, a 4x4, a V8 coupe, a sleeper wagon, a pickup, a muscle car and a rally hatch), each with its own live engine sound. Drive any of them around the ranch and the country on a laptop or a phone, or look them over in the model bay, on a turntable or all parked together in a garage, with their hoods open. |
