# Car games

| Game | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Dirt Sleeper](dirt-sleeper/) | Build one stock-looking luxury SUV into a dirt-drag sleeper or a mud rig, then race it on a ranch. |

| [Redline County: Wide Open 2: Road Not Found + Traffic](rlcwo2rnf-traffic/) | Being built: Wide Open 2 with life on the roads (traffic on Highway 40 and the town's streets, parked cars, people at the markers) and the map with waypoints (pins, a waypoint by a tap, the route by road). |
