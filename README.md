# Car games

| Game | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Dirt Sleeper](dirt-sleeper/) | Build one stock-looking luxury SUV into a dirt-drag sleeper or a mud rig, then race it on a ranch. |
| [Dirt Sleeper Showdown](dirt-sleeper-showdown/) | Dirt Sleeper HD with cross-country races over mixed ground, rivals who size your car up by its looks (and pay a bonus when you beat a car that looks faster), and callouts at the drag strip. |
