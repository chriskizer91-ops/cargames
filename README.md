# Car games

| Game | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Dirt Sleeper](dirt-sleeper/) | Build one stock-looking luxury SUV into a dirt-drag sleeper or a mud rig, then race it on a ranch. |
| [Rut Access: Road Not Found](rut-access/) | Being built: Dirt Sleeper Showdown's driving, cars and races, with Mini Dirt Run Solo's machines, drawn by Shiftlight 3's renderer for a phone, with night, rain and fog. |
| [Redline County: Wide Open 2: Road Not Found](wide-open-2/) | Being built: the sequel to Redline County: Wide Open (the county, its town, salt, park, career, challenges, people and races), drawn by Shiftlight 3's renderer for a phone, and run top to bottom for bugs on the way. |
| [Redline County: Wide Open 2: Road Not Found + Traffic](rlcwo2rnf-traffic/) | Being built: Wide Open 2 with life on the roads (traffic on Highway 40 and the town's streets, parked cars, people at the markers) and the map with waypoints (pins, a waypoint by a tap, the route by road). |
| [Redline County: SUV Studio](suv-studio/) | Being built: the Road Not Found stock truck alone on a studio backdrop, modelled part by part for a close look (wheels, lamps, grille, cabin), from the game's own car builders. |
| [SUV: Super Unlimited Venture](suv-venture/) | A test: the stock truck on the game's physics on a patch of ground in the owner's pictures, drawn three ways (the game's truck, the SUV Studio's, and the game's wearing the Studio's detail baked into pictures), with the frame rate, the GPU's time and a benchmark on screen. |
| [Redline County: Baked SUV Test](baked-suv-test/) | A test: Road Not Found + Traffic with the stock truck drawn baked (SUV Studio's detailed truck baked into three pictures on the game's own mesh), switchable in Settings against the game's own. |
| [Redline County: Salt Mini](salt-mini/) | A mini: the SUV Studio's full truck on the game's physics (driven as in SUV), a bare salt flat with a ten-mile road, a speedometer, nitrous and the tuner bay. No sound yet. |
