    How the money works

    Every feat pays: drifts, jumps, two wheels, saving it from a roll, speed runs on dirt and gravel, burnouts, close calls past the trees, splashes, the mud pit, Red Hill, the rock garden, laps and clock runs. Do them back to back and they stack into a combo: the multiplier grows with every feat and more with every new kind. The combo banks after a few quiet seconds. Roll it, wreck it or reset before then and the unbanked money is gone.
