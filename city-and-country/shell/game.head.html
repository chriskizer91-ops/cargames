    How the money works

    Every feat pays: drifts, jumps, two wheels, saving it from a roll, speed runs on dirt and gravel, burnouts, close calls past the trees, splashes, the mud pit, Red Hill, the rock garden, laps and clock runs. Do them back to back and they stack into a combo: the multiplier grows with every feat and more with every new kind. The combo banks after a few quiet seconds. Roll it, wreck it or reset before then and the unbanked money is gone.

    Pitch (V while driving)

    Keyboard gas

    Pitch is a test: the synth is told the engine turns 10 or 20% slower than it does, so the note drops; the tach, the shifts and the speed stay true. Keyboard gas: Like a foot is half gas while you hold W, so the automatic shifts around 3,500 like a truck driven normally; tap W twice quickly to floor it (a kickdown) until you let go; Floor it is all or nothing, every gear to the redline. 1 to 9 always give that much gas.

    Developer: traction

    For tuning while the game is made; the finished game won't have this. Traction is the ground, not the cars. It sets how much grip each surface gives, and every car feels that through its own tyres, so the cars keep their order: on dirt the drag car's slicks have less grip than the crawler's mud tyres at any setting, and on the drag strip they have more. The handling knobs work on every car, each from its own setup. Changes take hold as you drive and are remembered. T while driving steps Traction through 75, 100, 125 and 150%.
